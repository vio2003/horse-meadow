Modified from the original: the pack ships 24 animation clips and this game
plays three, so Death, Gun_Shoot, Sword_Slash, Punch, Kick, Roll and the rest
were stripped and the file repacked, leaving Idle/Walk/Run. That is ~40% of the
bytes, and every byte here is precached to the iPad for offline play.
Geometry, materials and the 62-bone rig are untouched.

Kept here as a courtesy record of where it came from.
